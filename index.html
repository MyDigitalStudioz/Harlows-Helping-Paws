The Story

Two days of helping paws

After the adventure in book one, Lois has retired — and retirement doesn’t suit her for long. With plenty of pep still in her step, she sets out to help her community. Animals, humans, or maybe both? Harlow tags along to find out.

Day One

The Snuggle Inn

A cage-free animal shelter where Lois volunteers and Harlow makes the rounds: bunnies Cocoa & Shadow, a mama cat and her five “spice girl” kittens, Howard the retired mountain-rescue Saint Bernard, three ponies, two gentle retired circus tigers — and two beagle puppies who find their forever home.

Day Two

The Community Hope Shelter

Harlow and Lois hop into the cool old ’65 Mustang and head across town, where a warm meal is served free to anyone who’s hungry. Harlow explores game rooms and clothing racks, earns the best chin scratches ever from Grandpa, assists Nurse Mira on her rounds — and politely declines one very large blue knitted hat.

The Lead Cast

Portraits, locked

Every character in a Lois Farrell book starts as a locked recipe — an exact description of who they are and how they look — before a single page is drawn. The three leads of book two are final: same faces, same finish, on every page to come.

Harlow

The star. Seal-point Ragdoll, azure-cerulean eyes, and that very long plume tail.

Lois

The author herself, in story form — deep-plum cardigan, silver bow brooch, ready to help.

Nurse Mira

All in pink at the Community Hope Shelter — scrub cap, stethoscope, and the warmest smile on rounds.
