The Story

Two days of helping paws

After the adventure in book one, Lois has retired — and retirement doesn’t suit her for long. With plenty of pep still in her step, she sets out to help her community. Animals, humans, or maybe both? Harlow tags along to find out.

Day One

The Snuggle Inn

A cage-free animal shelter where Lois volunteers and Harlow makes the rounds: bunnies Cocoa & Shadow, a mama cat and her five “spice girl” kittens, Howard the retired mountain-rescue Saint Bernard, three ponies, two gentle retired circus tigers — and two beagle puppies who find their forever home.

Day Two

The Community Hope Shelter

Harlow and Lois hop into the cool old ’65 Mustang and head across town, where a warm meal is served free to anyone who’s hungry. Harlow explores game rooms and clothing racks, earns the best chin scratches ever from Grandpa, assists Nurse Mira on her rounds — and politely declines one very large blue knitted hat.

The Lead Cast

Portraits, locked

Every character in a Lois Farrell book starts as a locked recipe — an exact description of who they are and how they look — before a single page is drawn. These are the cast’s reference portraits — same faces, same finish, on every page to come.

Harlow

Our narrator — a curious, kind-hearted cat with a happy purr and a nose for a good story.

The star. Seal-point Ragdoll, azure-cerulean eyes, and that very long plume tail.

Lois

Newly retired after 50 years of work, and already looking for her next way to help.

The author herself, in story form — deep-plum cardigan, silver bow brooch, ready to help.

Nurse Mira

Dressed all in pink, checking on everyone at the Hope Shelter to keep them happy and healthy.

All in pink at the Community Hope Shelter — scrub cap, stethoscope, and the warmest smile on rounds.

Maria

Warm and kind — big joyful grin, winged eyeliner, and a soft spot for yellow daisies.

Mia

Helping hands at the Community Hope Shelter clothing exchange — navy henley, jeans, royal-blue sneakers, and her white smartwatch.

Jon

Steady and warm — silver in his hair, white chin beard, always ready to help.

The Supporting Cast

More friends of the Inn

The supporting cast joins the same recipe-first way — a dialed-in recipe, a headshot, and a full body each. Howard is the first one home.

Howard

A gentle Saint Bernard who once rescued lost hikers from deep mountain snow.

The retired mountain-rescue Saint Bernard of The Snuggle Inn — a gentle old hero, warm and proud, exactly where he wants to be.

Yuki

A white tiger retired from the circus, loving every sunny nap.

The snowy white tiger — a retired circus sweetheart who purrs loudest in a patch of warm sun.

Raja

An orange tiger retired from the circus, loving every sunny nap.

The bright orange tiger — a gentle retired circus storyteller who greets his friends with a soft, friendly chuff.

Cocoa

One of two bunnies from a closed-down farm, now living the cozy life at The Snuggle Inn.

The caramel bunny — half of The Snuggle Inn’s welcoming committee, a former farm bunny who greets every newcomer with a twitching nose.

Shadow

One of two bunnies from a closed-down farm, now living the cozy life at The Snuggle Inn.

The charcoal bunny — the other half of the welcoming committee, all happy hops, with one flopped ear and a plaid scarf for every occasion.

Ginger

A proud mama cat and her five kittens: Pepper, Saffron, Sugar, Hania, and Sesame.

The orange tabby mama of The Snuggle Inn — rescued from life outside with her kittens, and now, in her words, treated like royalty.

The Spice Girls

Ginger’s five kittens: Pepper, Saffron, Sugar, Hania, and Sesame.

Five sisters, five coats — a chorus of tiny meows at The Snuggle Inn, and the prettiest kittens ever, per their mama.

Ginger the Pony

A chestnut pony who leads the games in the sunny field at The Snuggle Inn.

The trio’s greeter and spokesperson — first to say hello with a happy neigh, and squealing with delight when the bag of apples and carrots comes out.

Penny

A black pony who loves kicking up her heels in the fresh air.

The calm, gentle middle sister of the pony trio — content to let Ginger do the talking while she walks the sunny field.

Freckles

A spotted pony, the smallest of the three, and always mid-prance.

The playful little spark of the trio — white coat, chestnut spots, silvery mane, and heels forever kicking up.

Grandpa

A friendly guest at The Community Hope Shelter, with a big smile and the best hugs in the building.

He called Harlow right over to say hello — and he gives the BEST hugs and chin scratches ever, before settling into his cozy chair in the common room.

Joe

A volunteer in The Community Hope Shelter kitchen, dishing up the yummy feast with Lois and Maria.

One of the kind helpers serving the free home-cooked meal — navy sweater, sage apron, and a warm welcome for everyone in line.

Tony

A boy staying at The Community Hope Shelter — and the proud owner of brand-new fuzzy pajamas.

On the book’s last night, Harlow curled up right beside him in his nice, warm bed and snuggled close until he drifted off to dreamland.

The Newman Family

The sweet family who came to The Snuggle Inn looking for a puppy — and went home with two.

Mom, dad, and their little girl — they stopped to pet and play with the pups, smiled big smiles, and chose Pixie and Spike to be part of their family forever.

Pixie

A hopeful little beagle puppy who wished on the Newman family — and they chose her.

The pair’s little spokesperson — wide white blaze, broken black saddle, face tipped up in a hopeful pant.

Spike

Pixie’s playmate, a bold little beagle puppy in a bandit mask, with a grin for everyone.

The bold one of the puppy pair — dark bandit mask, full black blanket saddle, chin level with a closed-mouth grin, sitting up proud.

The ’65 Mustang

Lois’s classic 1965 Ford Mustang convertible in Silver Blue — top down, honeycomb grille, pale blue-gray interior, back for Book 2.
