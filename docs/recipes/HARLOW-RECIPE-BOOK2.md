## §2 Book-2 amendments (Jon's picks, 2026-11-19 — these refine §1 where the favorites speak)

1. **Body color:** the body reads **visibly warm caramel-brown** (the 2026-11-06 cover correction), deepening to chocolate on the legs, cap, and tail. Cream survives only as sunlit highlight on the flanks and belly — never a mostly-cream cat.
2. **Ruff and tail mass:** Harlow wears a **full, lush neck ruff** and the tail is a **signature plume** — long, thick, dark chocolate-brown, roughly as wide as the body when curled beside him (all four favorites agree). Plush volume is built from **broad grouped tufts**, never stringy individual hairs. (This supersedes book-1's "refined rather than excessively shaggy" — Jon's favorites are lusher.)
3. **Eyes:** bright **azure-cerulean blue** — the most luminous note in any frame — with a lighter cyan gradient toward the lower iris, depth only at the rim, glossy surface and bright catchlights, and a **small natural sliver of white visible at the eye corners** (Jon's call, 2026-11-19, checked against the book-1 close-ups). Never deep royal or navy; the iris never fills the whole eye edge-to-edge. Proportions stay at the canonical gentle enlargement; no anime widening.
4. **Finish:** "polished storybook cast finish" in §1 now means §5's THE LOOK, word for word. Where book-1 language ("gentle painted shading," "subtle cream paper texture") pulled renders toward pale watercolor, §5 governs.

## §3 Markings map (boundaries — match in every pose)

- **White:** whisker pads and muzzle; lower cheeks; a **continuous white blaze** rising up the nose bridge into the center of the forehead; chin, flowing into the generous white chest bib/ruff underside; **white mitts on all four paws**.
- **Seal-brown:** cap and medium triangular ears; surround around the eyes — but the dark must **never** close over the white muzzle, lower cheeks, or nose bridge as an uninterrupted mask.
- **Body:** warm caramel-brown back, shoulders, sides, hindquarters; darker chocolate-brown legs ending in the white mitts; tail fully dark chocolate-brown.
- **Details:** pink triangular nose; inner ears muted warm pink-brown; fine white whiskers; dark pupils with small bright catchlights.

## §4 Build, expression, pose

- **Build:** sturdy adult Ragdoll — substantial body, balanced head, strong shoulders. Not a kitten, not slim, not flat-faced. (Book-1 build note stands: "sleek/lean" in a scene note means the feline line under the plush coat, never a slimmed silhouette.)
- **Expression default:** calm, confident, regal, with a hint of warmth. Relaxed attentive blue eyes; tiny natural closed-mouth smile. No angry/worried brow, no teeth, no tongue, no human grin, no decorative eyelashes.
- **Accessories:** none. No collar, tags, bow, clothing, crown.
- **Hero pose (cast portrait):** full body sitting tall in three-quarter view, upright chest, front paws resting naturally together, hind paws beside them, head turned gently toward the viewer, tail in a graceful full curve beside the body. Dignified and feline — no raised human-like paws.

## §5 THE LOOK — the Helping Paws finish (BOOK-2 STYLE LOCK — paste into every character recipe and every spread prompt)

> **Polished dimensional storybook finish.** Rich, lush digital storybook painting with sculpted, softly rounded forms and **dimensional cartoon lighting** — a clear warm golden-hour key light, amber rim light tracing the fur silhouette, soft warm shadows. Fur and hair render as **broad grouped tufts with flowing direction**, deep chocolate underlayers and warm cream-gold highlights — plush and painterly, never individual photoreal strands. Eyes are glossy and luminous with bright catchlights. Palette is warm and saturated: caramel amber, chocolate brown, cream, with one vivid accent color per character (Harlow's is sapphire blue). **Backgrounds** are softer and simpler than the characters — gently painted, warm, slightly diffused — so the cast always reads one level more polished than the world behind them. Every character in every scene uses this exact finish.

**NEVER (the drift list — any one of these in a render = fail, regenerate):**
- Flat watercolor washes, visible wash edges, or paper-grain texture **on a character**
- Pale, desaturated, "soft and gentle" rendering that loses the golden richness
- Glossy plastic 3D-render surfaces or video-game fur
- Photorealism or individual-hair strand detail
- Flat anime cel shading or sharp vector outlines
- A character whose finish differs from the rest of the cast in the same frame
