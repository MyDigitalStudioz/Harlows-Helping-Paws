# Lois board — Jon's one-pass ChatGPT recipe (2026-11-20) — THE BOARD METHOD

Jon made the adopted Lois board himself in ChatGPT in ONE generation pass. Trinity's
plate-and-paste pipeline (v3–v12) never matched it: every composite defect (seams,
fills, window caps, glyph errors) came from the split. **This recipe is now the
primary method for character boards** (Jon's ruling, 2026-11-20). Governed-parts
assembly remains the fallback when a one-pass face drifts.

## The four references (attach in this order — each has a JOB)
1. Lois close-up portrait (canonical) — FACE MASTER: brows, eyes, cheek fullness,
   nose, smile, age lines, hairline, bob shape.
2. Lois standing full body (canonical) — BODY MASTER: proportions, hero pose,
   wardrobe, how clothes hang.
3. Harlow character sheet (approved board) — LAYOUT TEMPLATE: grid, panel sizes,
   hierarchy, headings, parchment style. Also Harlow's design for cameos.
4. Earlier Lois board — SECONDARY: expression/action ideas, detail close-ups,
   color labels. Where it differs from 1–2, follow 1–2. For sizing/spacing, follow 3.

Method: attach all four uncropped, paste the verbatim prompt, ONE complete board
in a single pass, artwork + lettering generated together, opaque background.
No layout assembly, no retouching. Then gate: hero vs standing portrait, Front
head shot vs close-up at full size; side views + expressions for consistent nose,
cheeks, jaw, hairline; labels + footer legible.

## Verbatim generation prompt (Jon's, as used)
Create ONE complete landscape 3:2 illustrated character model sheet for LOIS. The four attached images in order: 1 Lois closeup is authoritative facial likeness; 2 full body Lois is authoritative proportions, hero pose and wardrobe; 3 Harlow cat board is the exact graphic design template whose grid, sizes of subjects relative to panels, visual hierarchy, headings and illustrated parchment style to echo as closely as humanly possible; 4 prior Lois sheet is secondary for pose ideas, palette and details. Build an illustrated companion board to image 3. Keep all outside margins and bottom footer visible. Aim for the highest achievable resolution and text legibility.

Faithfully preserve Lois from reference 1 in every angle and panel: woman in her 70s, kind smallish deep warm brown eyes with natural lower lids and crow's feet, warm softly rounded cheeks, same distinctive nose bridge and rounded tip, asymmetrical warm tooth-showing smile in hero, matching jaw and natural age lines. Her defining short jaw-length side-swept waved bob has soft sculpted S-waves, brunette base with warm brownish-blonde highlights, high sweep in front and rounded curved sides, never long. Match exact colors and proportions of reference 2: long deep plum rib-knit open cardigan with two patch pockets and antique brass buttons, small silver bow brooch on her right chest (viewer left in front view), warm taupe crewneck, soft black trousers, brown leather knee boots. Natural mature proportions approximately 5'4". Keep forehead, eye placement, cheek shape and hairline consistent in all views. Physically plausible hands and feet. Polished dimensional animated storybook illustration, finely grouped forms, soft clean shading, warm natural light and subtle rim, warm ivory paper and delicately painted florals around margins, rich dark umber lettering. Reproduce the Harlow board layout very closely, using full-size readable subjects instead of tiny clustered figures from the old Lois sheet.

Harlow board grid: upper left around 39% width and 46% height: oversized flowing brown script "Lois" like Harlow's, small heart accent, bold serif "CHARACTER SHEET", same muted teal ribbon "DIMENSIONAL ANIMATED STORYBOOK". Brief checklist on left side under ribbon: "Lois Farrell", "Harlow's person", "Warm & kind", "Brown eyes", "Side-swept wavy bob", "Plum cardigan", "Silver bow brooch". Large full-body hero next to the checklist within the upper-left block, reference 2 stance, one hand tucked in pocket, boots fully visible, face large enough to identify, small illustrated garden setting behind her.
Upper center, immediately to the right of hero and left of details column: "HEAD SHOTS", five equally sized individual face panels that fill their cells, captions "FRONT", "THREE QUARTER", "LEFT PROFILE", "RIGHT PROFILE", "HAPPY". Directly below those in same central width "TURNAROUND / MODEL SHEET", four large evenly spaced same-scale full body models with common ground and neutral arms at sides, "FRONT", "LEFT SIDE", "BACK", "RIGHT SIDE". Opposite true profiles, full boots always visible, brooch only where physically visible.
Far right narrow column from top through action rows: "DETAILS", stack eight close-up crops and their captions, "BROWN EYE", "NOSE", "SMILE", "HAIR SWEEP", "BACK OF BOB", "SILVER BOW", "KNIT & BUTTON", "LEATHER BOOT".
Middle left below hero: "EXPRESSIONS", eight distinct separated portrait tiles filling their cells in a 4 by 2 array, top "GENTLE SMILE", "HAPPY", "LAUGHING", "CURIOUS"; bottom "SURPRISED", "CONCERNED", "AFFECTIONATE", "PROUD".
Middle center next to expressions below turnaround: "ACTIONS / POSES", eight distinct tiles in 4 by 2 array with readable figures. Top "WALKING WITH HARLOW", "SITTING WITH HARLOW", "HOLDING A KITTEN", "COOKING"; bottom "READING ALOUD" (with a child), "CARRYING A BASKET", "DRIVING THE MUSTANG" (Silver Blue 1965 top-down convertible), "WAVING HELLO". Harlow appears only in small supporting roles where called for, with his signature blue eyes, white blaze and mitts, and dark plush tail.
Bottom row from left to right: "COLOR PALETTE" with eight painted labeled swatches "DEEP BROWN", "GOLDEN BROWN", "WARM SKIN", "ROSY CHEEK", "DEEP PLUM", "TAUPE", "SOFT BLACK", "BOOT BROWN". "SIZE & PROPORTIONS" with a standing Lois, a simple dimension arrow and caption "Approx. 5'4"", natural adult proportions. "KEY TRAITS TO MAINTAIN" with green checkmarks and short lines "Warm brown eyes", "Soft cheeks and natural smile lines", "Jaw-length side-swept waves", "Plum cardigan and silver bow", "Black trousers and brown knee boots", "Same likeness in every view". Bottom footer "STYLE GUIDE — Dimensional animated storybook • Warm natural light • Preserve Lois's likeness in every view." Design should read as the sister board to Harlow's, with restrained foliage, clean thin rule separators, dark readable text, no extra copy and strong visual hierarchy. Output the design as a clean edge-to-edge illustrated sheet, not a mockup, no hands holding it, no skew.

  nose/chin from angles the portraits don't show. Portraits 1–2 decide any drift dispute.
