## §1 Identity — NEVER CHANGE

> NEVER CHANGE: Mira's exact facial identity as in the six nurse photographs — the slightly long oval face with soft jaw and gently rounded chin; the strong, dark, thick, fairly straight brows; the large dark-brown natural eyes with no makeup look; the medium nose at its real size; the wide genuine smile showing natural upper teeth with slight real variation and slight asymmetry; the warm medium-brown skin, even in tone; the very dark brown long wavy hair. Do not beautify her, glamour-makeup her, slim her face, shrink her nose, perfect her teeth, or substitute a generic pretty nurse. The small natural imperfections are what make it her.

## §3 Face map (match in every view)

- Slightly long oval face, soft jawline, gently rounded chin — never round/full-cheeked, never slimmed.
- Strong dark brows, thick and fairly straight — a signature feature, keep them prominent.
- Large dark-brown eyes, bright and wide, natural — no mascara glamour, no eyeliner look, short natural lashes.
- Medium nose, straight bridge, rounded natural tip — never a small button nose.
- Wide genuine smile, upper teeth showing, natural variation in the teeth, slight asymmetry (warmer on one side) — never a perfect uniform row.
- Warm medium-brown skin, even tone — no blush, no orange oversaturation.
- Very dark brown hair, long loose waves flowing from under the cap past her shoulders — rendered in the §5 grouped-tuft finish, never photoreal strands.

## §4 Build, expression, pose

- **Build:** natural young-adult proportions (she is 20), head ≈ 1/7.5 of height — never oversized (the Lois Mustang-panel lesson).
- **Expression default:** her big warm smile from the photographs — joyful, gentle, kind. Nurse Mira "checks that everyone is happy and healthy" (Lois's cast draft) — warmth is her character note.
- **Close-up portrait pose:** head-and-shoulders, gentle three-quarter view, eyes warmly toward the viewer, slight natural head tilt, cap and hair fully in frame.
- **Full-body pose:** standing relaxed, gentle smile toward the viewer, complete outfit head to sneakers inside the frame.

## §5 THE LOOK — the Helping Paws finish (BOOK-2 STYLE LOCK — paste into every character recipe and every spread prompt)

> **Polished dimensional storybook finish.** Rich, lush digital storybook painting with sculpted, softly rounded forms and **dimensional cartoon lighting** — a clear warm golden-hour key light, amber rim light tracing the fur silhouette, soft warm shadows. Fur and hair render as **broad grouped tufts with flowing direction**, deep chocolate underlayers and warm cream-gold highlights — plush and painterly, never individual photoreal strands. Eyes are glossy and luminous with bright catchlights. Palette is warm and saturated: caramel amber, chocolate brown, cream, with one vivid accent color per character (Mira's is hot pink). **Backgrounds** are softer and simpler than the characters — gently painted, warm, slightly diffused — so the cast always reads one level more polished than the world behind them. Every character in every scene uses this exact finish.

**NEVER (the drift list — any one of these in a render = fail, regenerate):**
- Flat watercolor washes, visible wash edges, or paper-grain texture **on a character**
- Pale, desaturated, "soft and gentle" rendering that loses the golden richness
- Glossy plastic 3D-render surfaces or video-game fur
- Photorealism or individual-hair strand detail
- Flat anime cel shading or sharp vector outlines
- A character whose finish differs from the rest of the cast in the same frame
- **Mira-specific:** beautified/genericized face (see §1) — a "prettier stranger" is a fail even if the finish is perfect
