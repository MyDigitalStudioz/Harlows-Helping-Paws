## §5 THE LOOK — the Helping Paws finish (BOOK-2 STYLE LOCK — paste into every character recipe and every spread prompt)

> **Polished dimensional storybook finish.** Rich, lush digital storybook painting with sculpted, softly rounded forms and **dimensional cartoon lighting** — a clear warm golden-hour key light, amber rim light tracing the fur silhouette, soft warm shadows.
