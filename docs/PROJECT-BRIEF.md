## The story (Draft 1, ~1,926 words)

- **Opening:** Harlow narrates. After the first book's adventure, Lois retired in June 2025 after 50 years of work. She loved being home with Harlow at first, then got antsy — she still had pep in her step and wanted to help her community. Animals, humans, or maybe both?
- **Day One — The Snuggle Inn:** a cage-free animal shelter. Lois volunteers; Harlow tags along. Meets Cocoa & Shadow (bunnies from a closed-down farm), Ginger the mama cat and her five "spice girl" kittens (Pepper, Saffron, Sugar, Hania, Sesame), Howard the Saint Bernard (retired mountain-rescue dog), three ponies (Ginger, Penny, Freckles), tigers Yuki & Raja (retired circus), and beagle puppies Pixie & Spike, who are adopted by the Newman family.
- **Day Two — The Community Hope Shelter:** Harlow and Lois drive the cool old '65 Mustang across town. A free home-cooked meal for anyone who's hungry; Grandpa and the best chin scratches ever; Nurse Mira doing health rounds; a clothing room where Mia puts a too-big bright blue knitted hat on Harlow.

## Production roadmap

1. **Story** — NOW. Lois polishes wording; story lock.
2. **Edit** — spread plan, page-by-page text, clean production text.
3. **Cast & Recipes** — character recipes + reference portraits for every new face (Harlow's recipe carries over from book one).
4. **Illustration** — spread art in the book-one hand, reviewed spread by spread.
5. **Layout & Proof** — typeset book, printed proof, Lois's notes pass.
6. **Print & Publish** — Lulu hardcover + paperback, digital flipbook.

## Rules carried over from book one (standing)

- **Lois's side stays light** — she writes and notes; Jon runs production. Never bog her down with decisions or technical detail.
- **The manuscript is Lois's** — draft changes come from her, never rewritten by the agent.
- **Notes to Lois's Muse** go in the ONE shared inbox doc (Mom's Notes Drive folder), newest first.
- **Production canon:** the Picture-Book Production Playbook (see `HARVEST-FROM-BOOK-1.md`) — recipes + approved portraits, never brief from memory.
- **Standing commands (book one) will be mirrored when this book reaches build:** "Build new book / Publish book / Patch book" pattern.
- **Proofs first:** the printed proof is the verdict, not the file.
