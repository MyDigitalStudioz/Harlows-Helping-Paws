## Shape

- 15 spreads, pages 1–30, narrated by Harlow.
- Premise: Lois retired in June 2025 after 50 years of work, got antsy at home, and looks for a way to help her community — animals, humans, or both.
- Day 1 (pages 1–18): volunteering at The Snuggle Inn, a cage-free animal shelter.
- Day 2 (pages 19–30): volunteering at The Community Hope Shelter — free meals, health checks, warm clothes, and beds.
- Closes with a sequel tease: a big amusement park nearby where kitties come out and play at night.
